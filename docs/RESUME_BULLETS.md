# Resume Bullet Options

## Recommended

**Growth Funnel & Retention Analytics — Fashion E-commerce**  
Built a Myntra-inspired interactive growth dashboard over **895K customer journeys and 816K successful orders**, modeling AARRR, D7–D90 repeat-purchase retention and promo cohorts; found an apparent **18.1% relative D30 retention gap** for promo-acquired buyers shrank to **~0.5 pp after cohort-month adjustment**, preventing a misleading discount-retention conclusion and informing a lifecycle experiment.

## Shorter

Built a Myntra-inspired growth analytics dashboard across **895K journeys / 816K orders**; analyzed funnel and D7–D90 cohorts, showing a raw **18.1% promo D30 retention penalty** fell to **~0.5 pp after cohort adjustment**, and designed a loyalty/lifecycle experiment.

## Product/marketing-heavy

Mapped a fashion-commerce AARRR funnel and retention system across **100K customers**, identifying purchase #2 as the highest-leverage lifecycle point; disproved a naive “discounts hurt retention” read by reducing a **3.7 pp raw D30 gap to ~0.5 pp** after cohort standardization, then built an interactive experiment-sizing model.

## Analytics-heavy

Joined customer, clickstream, product and transaction data to analyze **895K sessions and 852K transactions**; built censored D7/D30/D60/D90 repeat-purchase cohorts and acquisition-month standardized promo analysis, revealing substantial cohort-mix confounding in the raw retention result.

## Do not use

Avoid phrasing such as:

> “Recommended referral and loyalty nudges projected to improve 30-day retention by 18%.”

The dataset cannot causally support that statement.

If you want the number on the resume, use:

> “Built an 18% relative retention-lift scenario model for experiment sizing.”

That accurately describes what was modeled.
