05 / Growth actions

Five decisions I would take to product & CRM.

Recommendations are tied to observed behavior, with causal claims reserved for experiments.

01

Accelerate purchase #2

For first-time promo buyers, trigger personalized category recommendations around D7–D14 and a loyalty milestone before D30. The near-term repeat rate is the clearest headroom.

Primary KPI: D30 repeat purchase
02

Don’t cut promos from the raw gap

The 3.7 pp raw retention gap collapses to ~0.5 pp after month-standardization. Treat “promo causes churn” as a hypothesis, not a conclusion.

Method: randomized holdout
03

Build a second-purchase loyalty loop

Long-run repeat is high, suggesting habit can form. Reward the second successful purchase rather than issuing blanket acquisition discounts.

Guardrail: discount cost / buyer
04

Instrument referral properly

Add invite_sent, invite_opened, invite_accepted, referrer_id and referred_order events before calculating referral conversion or K-factor.

AARRR gap: Referral
05

Upgrade acquisition attribution

“Mobile vs Web” is too coarse and performs almost identically. Add campaign, referrer, paid/organic and creative IDs so CAC can be linked to retention quality.

Next metric: retained CAC
06 / Methodology

Designed to survive an interview follow-up.

Every headline metric has an explicit definition, maturity rule, and limitation.

Dataset

4-table public e-commerce dataset

Customer, product, clickstream and transaction tables. Raw data is not redistributed in this repository; only aggregated outputs are included.

D30 retention

Second successful purchase by day 30

Only customers whose first successful purchase is at least 30 days before the dataset end are included.

Sale cohort

Promo on first successful purchase

A customer is “promo-acquired” when promo_amount > 0 on their first successful transaction.

Adjustment

Acquisition-month standardization

Promo and non-promo D30 rates are compared within first-purchase month, then weighted by promo cohort size.

Funnel caveat

Transaction-centered clickstream

95%+ journeys reach booking, so these rates are not representative of a normal top-of-funnel storefront.

Referral caveat

Not observable

No invite/referrer linkage exists. Referral recommendations are product hypotheses, not retrospective measured findings.

Data quality

Join coverage is extremely high
